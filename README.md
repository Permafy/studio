# Credits
Here are some credits for the other Scratch Mods/Reddit Posts/etc that helped developing [Permafy](https://permafy.org), (they are open source, go support them too!):

## TurboWarp
https://turbowarp.org/ or https://turbowarp.github.io/
Used for compiling projects to JavaScript/JS. Very cool, we also used it as a base and of course for the Topbar color.

## PenguinMod
https://penguinmod.com/ or https://penguinmod.github.io/
Used as a base and for the extensions, new blocks and very awesome things such as ALMOST THE ENTIRETY OF THE WEBSITE (son)

## NitroBolt
https://nitrobolt.org/
Used for the Assets tab.

## Scratch Addons
https://scratchaddons.com/
Used for the Addons.

## Dash/DashBlocks
https://dashblocks.org/ or https://dashblocks.github.io/
Used for some of the Topbar features, some other features, a sort of banner that welcomes you, and other cool features. Also gave me an idea to put a "Recent Commits/Pushes or What's new square on the menu".

## Gandi IDE
i dont really know what i used for, (i didnt use it for anything, probably helped penguinmod or other ones because i dont remember using gandi ide but im still going to put it to credit them.)

https://snail-ide.js.org/ or https://snail-ide.com/ or https://snailshare.vercel.app/
Used for the Blocks, some features, and the menu colored texts (Built off of Scratch, TurboWarp...). Also gave me the same idea Dash/DashBlocks gave me (Also gave me an idea to put a "Recent Commits/Pushes or What's new square on the menu".)

## GaiaMod
https://gaiamod-main.github.io/
Used for the Topbar, the menu colored texts, and the Screenshot and Record features.
